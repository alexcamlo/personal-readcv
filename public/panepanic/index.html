      Settings

      Hold Arrow keys or WASD to move; release to stop. On the Window, swipe at least 18 pixels and hold to keep moving. Swipe again to steer; lift to stop.

      The golden fire spirit is your Player. The solid, light-and-dark Boundary is safe. Turns happen at intersections. A stopped partial path finishes before turning. Near-misses that merely look like contact are safe.

      Move into the frost to draw a glowing, vulnerable Trail. Reconnect with any Boundary to close it and reveal part of the Window away from the Frost Wisp. Retrace your Trail exactly to erase it. A Trail cannot touch or cross itself. Each Level asks you to clear part of the Window, restore marked Zones, or both.

      The Frost Wisp wanders the frost (~) and reflects off Boundaries. An exposed Trail starts a Warning (!): after 0.75 seconds the Wisp pursues (») the nearest of you and your Trail. Touching you or any Trail segment immediately fails the Run. Failure is announced, then the same Level setup resets automatically with a fresh countdown; there are no lives.

      Pause freezes the Run. After an interruption, choose Resume and give a fresh movement command. Escape pauses while the Window has focus.

      Sound is optional: every cue is also visible, and “Sound” mutes. “Reduced motion” makes capture and full-reveal fades instant, like your system's reduced-motion setting.
